What the categories mean

Listed open

Explicit listed interval includes the selected time.

Listed closed

Known schedule excludes the selected time, or the day is explicitly closed.

Conditional

Access outside an explicit base interval depends on season, daylight, permits, or another unresolved condition.

Hours unknown

The selected day has missing, omitted, or unresolved schedule information.
